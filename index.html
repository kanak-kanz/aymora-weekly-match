Prototype · Aymora Tech Challenge

One introduction a week, chosen for everyone at once.

Aymora promises at least one meaningful match every week, with no quality compromise. Picking each person's top match doesn't keep that promise, because the same few people end up at the top of everyone's list. This engine scores compatibility, explains every introduction, and pairs the whole pool together each week so the promise holds.
